# v3.1 simulation provenance and limits

`sim/blackout_sim_v31.py` reuses the v3 model with explicit endowment, release schedule, supply split, zero actual ETH liquidity and useful-work costs. It runs 48 custom-path cases: four endowments × instant/14/42/56 days × steady/hype/weak. Every case has zero external ETH capital. `sim/heartbeat_sweep_v31.py` first replays recorded cash flows at 1 bps resolution, then reruns complete economic paths at a selected share. No program calls the network, reads keys or signs transactions. Results are deterministic for seed 20261011.

## Opening supply and market

The existing 1 billion tokens are conserved every six-hour step across AMM inventory, unsold contributor allocation, locked/released reward pool, reserved redistributions/prizes/scrap, player balances and queued withdrawals. Numerical error must remain below 0.02token. No token or ETH endowment is minted by the model.

Ten percent goes to approximately 900 contributor agents. Sale intent is 35%/50%/65% of that 100 million over the first 14 days in steady/hype/weak. Main case assumes a two-day claim lock, then uniform sale intent through day 14; actual lock terms must be read from the launch distributor. Lock 0/7 day sensitivities bracket that assumption. Contributor selling is independent of gameplay and NFT starter entitlements. The model retains 900 NFT starter entitlements as the v3 gameplay population assumption, arriving hour 48 through day 16; the actual NFT root/count must come from the eventual snapshot.

Opening cap is held at 11.111111111 ETH (the previous 10 ETH /90% implied cap), so spot is 10/900 million ETH/token in all options. AMM token shares 80/75/70/65% correspond to virtual ETH reserves 8.8889/8.3333/7.7778/7.2222. **Actual initial ETH is zero.** The virtual reserve is a constant-product approximation of single-sided launch liquidity, not capital. Actual spendable ETH is `x - openingVirtualX`. Sells stop at the initial token-inventory boundary; excess tokens remain in contributor/player wallets and are retried later. Their quoted value is never realized payback. Round-trip traders cannot create net liquidity. A concentrated Uniswap v4 position has a different curve; the actual launch range, fee, liquidity and spot must be tested separately.

The team transfers 100/150/200/250 million to the pool immediately at game readiness. Released principal accrues linearly over 14/42/56 days; instant availability is a comparator unsupported by production contracts. The model releases the current quarter-day's increment at the step, an epoch-granularity approximation. Actual activation can be delayed and starts the on-chain schedule at that later time. Remaining endowment stays locked after day 35 in 42/56 day runs. Buybacks and recycled game spends are available normally.

## Gameplay and economic policy

The horizon is 35 days/140epochs. Steady arrivals/day are 150 for three days then 80; hype is `600*exp(-day/5)+25`; weak is `40*exp(-day/15)+5`. Demand scales by `min(1,sqrt(21/currentPaybackForecast))`. New paid farms buy approximately `3*(.01/packPrice)^.7` packs, at least one, subject to inventory. These are behavioral assumptions, not observed demand.

Greedy equipment, repair/reinvestment, quarter-day withdrawal timing, referral aggregation, churn and event counterfactuals retain the [v3 assumptions](simulation-v3-historical.md). In particular, maintenance recycles 3% of net yield, restores 0.6health per step, and is not exact discrete repair pricing.65% cashout intent uses 0/3/7 day ages at 40/30/30 weights.40% referral adoption attributes aggregate extra 5% to the farm for conservation. Quitters stop mining, retain assets and finish queued cashouts. This presumes voluntary unplacement/retirement; permanently abandoned registered farms add real settlement costs. Expansion/merge/overclock/scrap *behavior* is not optimized or forecast. Solidity tests cover their unchanged actual accounting.

The custom path charges 2% project +0.25% network, plus capped aggregate sell pressure. The surcharge is a pressure proxy, not an exact minute-by-minute replay of V4Market's hour/day/TWAP expression. External round-trip volume is 50% of pack ETH. Slippage rejections, oracle attacks, transaction ordering, sequencer influence, concentrated liquidity and MEV are outside this economic approximation.

Planned cases start 3.5% daily emission and 15% electricity, moving in bounded daily steps toward 6% and 10% as in v3, with pace reductions and positive-weight responses. Negative severity is 5000 under timely swarm plans. `autonomous=True` retains default numeric policy and uses full negative severity 10000; it represents no swarm plan submissions while public bots still execute. It is not the same economic case as the tuned policy. The chain cannot supply its own transactions.

## Keeper cost model

Observed Robinhood RPC gas price was 20 million wei (0.02 gwei). The contract reimburses measured work plus 80k overhead with 25% margin, limited by a 50 million-wei ceiling, unit cap and actual heartbeat. The simulation estimates measurement gas per farm as 550k +70k × equipped items, credit 420k per farm plus 850k per epoch for closing the credit phase, audit 100k, entropy 120k per receipt, plan 210k, scoring 20k per participant, finalization 600k, buyback 2 million per 0.5 ETH transaction. Overhead is charged per actual modeled call: farm batches 4, score batches 64, each receipt separately, each buy batch separately. Calibration is compared with local full-farm tests; it is not a live gas guarantee. L1 data posting, contention, failed calls and gas spikes need additional operator margin.

The model records due and paid bounties separately, retains shortages, and checks cumulative income minus all due bounties and external run costs at **every** quarter-day. Empty work has no modeled payment. Phase ordering is aggregated within an epoch, so the first startup and intra-epoch race/profitability are not proven by positive end-of-step headroom. Real bots must inspect balances. Bounty caps bound expenditures but do not force public operators to accept uneconomic transactions.

The baseline 1 ETH cap is applied to actual heartbeat inflow, with excess redirected to reward buys. Base project fees remain 50/50 team/rewards; other streams are 13% team, variable heartbeat, and the balance rewards. Work costs affect reward buying through this routing feedback in full reruns. The minimum-share replay holds the original demand/price/work path fixed, so it is a candidate estimate only; use full reruns for a recommendation.

A policy schedule makes four IMD runs/day:0.5 IMD each,70 IMD over 35 days. Its illustrative ETH conversion is 0.003451 ETH/IMD, from the public market snapshot in `artifacts/v31/evidence`. Cost is 0.24157 ETH before payment/swap/bridge expenses. Price can change; multiply that line item by the actual quote ratio. The simulation records that expense separately, never withdraws it from the inaccessible heartbeat reserve, and reports team proceeds after external payment when assumed team-funded. Combined headroom is an economic capacity test, **not** a claim that the contract can pay the schedule.

## Interpreting results

Whole-cohort payback is the median of individual realized ETH recovery times over **all** paid farms joining that week, with unpaid/quit farms treated as right-censored. Conditional medians only among winners are supplementary. Not reached means fewer than half repaid within 35 days. Neither median says every player recovers spending. Cash includes withdrawal tax and execution price impact; retained/unfilled tokens never count. Player gas is not charged, so reported economic payback is optimistic by that amount.

Stall is the first day from day 3 with active farms below 25% of their past peak, or median live-farm forecast above 60 days. It is an economic-health threshold, not a contract halt. A weak scenario can remain mechanically active with very poor player recovery. Event loss is against a parallel no-negative-event health/destruction counterfactual at the same prices and budgets, retaining accumulated damage. It uses gross pre-electricity yield. Dividing by 0.70 gives a conservative after-tariff denominator at the 30% maximum tariff; the report must distinguish that stricter interpretation.

Only one deterministic seed is used for the main sweep. Demand, farming strategy, lock duration, launch liquidity shape and latency can dominate small differences between allocations. Results justify a comparison and identify misses; they are not evidence that a launch will be profitable or safe from price falls.
