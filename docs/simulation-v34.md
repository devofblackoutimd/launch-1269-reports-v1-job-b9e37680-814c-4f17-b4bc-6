# v3.4 economic experiment

Run `python3 sim/run_v34.py`. It uses only the standard library, deterministic seeds 20261011–20261015, 35 days (140 six-hour epochs), three demand environments (steady/hype/weak), and both 42/56-day drips. Thirty main runs plus ten steady-scenario lever comparisons produce [raw results](../artifacts/v34/simulation.json) and [median/range tables](../artifacts/v34/simulation.md). Each epoch reconciles the existing one-billion token supply; maximum conservation error is recorded. This is a scenario model derived from v3.3, not an EVM simulator or a forecast calibrated to live user telemetry.

The initial setup retains a single-sided liquidity model: 700M AMM tokens, 200M reward tokens, 100M swarm, **zero actual ETH capital**. Virtual ETH is `10*0.7/0.9`; users cannot sell beyond ETH subsequently entering that pool. No paid liquidity bootstrap, team loan or undisclosed external funding is assumed. The virtual curve/cap is a retained modeling assumption and must be compared with the actual launch implementation before launch. Starter entitlements remain 900 hypothetical snapshot claims over the existing window, not a new allocation.

## Arrivals, payback and accounting

Potential audience is the prior scenario's time-varying demand curve. Actual expected arrivals per epoch equal that audience / 4, multiplied by:

- price elasticity `clamp((0.01/currentPackPrice)^0.8,0.15,1.5)`;
- payback response `min(1.5,sqrt(14/max(1,observedPaybackDays)))`.

Observed payback is median paid surviving farms' acquisition cost divided by their last day's net mining value, with a 10-day prior before enough data exists. It is a behavioral signal, not realized return. Stochastic rounding converts expected arrivals into actual arrivals. Per-user pack counts also respond to current pack price. Churn retains the v3.3 yield-sensitive rule. Assumptions such as response elasticity, willingness to maintain/reinvest, hypothetical NFT-holder participation and holder sell schedules are not measured on-chain facts.

Payback outputs separately report whole-cohort realized median, fraction repaid and conditional median among only repaid wallets. If at least half the cohort has not repaid, the whole-cohort median is “not reached,” never replaced with a projected or survivor-only value. Week-2/3 entrants have shorter observation windows, explicitly retained as censored. Stall is the first modeled day >=3 with active farms below 25% of peak or observed payback above 60 days; null means none by day 35. Drawdown uses the maximum peak-to-trough token price loss. Team ETH includes the existing 10% pack/lobby/surcharge share and 50% base-project-fee share. Net team subtracts the inherited explicit external scheduler valuation of 0.5 IMD/run, four runs/day, at 0.003451 ETH/IMD: 0.24157 ETH total. It is not refreshed pricing, gas compensation or an authorized payment.

## v3.4 changes modeled

First installation uses a random remaining fraction of the joining epoch and just the first productive item. Later configuration remains next-epoch behavior. Epoch emission clips any empty prefix. Idle farms expire after 12 epochs; active players are assumed to sync daily. The model retains full discrete event/wear application at an epoch boundary, as the contract does; it is not continuous-time hardware simulation. Audit eligibility uses full raw hash before entry proration.

The regulator uses seven **completed** daily cash pack-sale ETH totals. Compare the last three days with the first three; target emission is `clamp(350*late/early,200,600)` bps/day. A zero/zero window targets the 200-bps floor; zero early with positive late targets 600. Move toward target by at most `current//10` per day and preserve the lower-runway safety reduction. For the first seven days, hold emission pending data. This is a swarm proposal constrained by Params, not a new privilege or an on-chain automated setter. Token-pack recycling and swap receipts are excluded from “fresh cash pack inflow.” Declining sales lower demand for emissions instead of triggering a payback-driven increase when new money is drying up. Tests check bounds, directions, cooldown eligibility and invalid histories.

Weekly best prizes use capped 1 ETH absolute spend and at least 20 active epochs. Ticket hash cap 100 in model units corresponds to 100,000 contract units; the weekly cap is 2,800 model units. District rewards require a spending participant; district score uses the soft 10% cap of uncapped total and active paying farms. Prize payout retains seven-day grace. Floating-point approximations do not reproduce Solidity integer dust or every deferred settlement schedule; contract tests validate those separately.

Buybacks have up to 72 five-minute opportunities per epoch. Each attempt is at most 0.5 ETH and at least `min(0.05 ETH, queued ETH)`. Batch size halves until a fill satisfies a 1.5% minimum output against six five-minute price observations; otherwise funds stay queued. Quotes deduct normal fees. A fixed 350k-gas successful buy scenario, transaction overhead, measured-work reimbursement cap and available heartbeat limit public-bot willingness. Successful-call count, rather than total ETH divided by maximum batch, determines gas overhead. Small uneconomic residuals stay queued. The six-point oracle is an approximation to the contract's minute observations: six-hour arrival/sell bursts and five-minute interpolation are model assumptions. It does not prove live oracle freshness, transaction ordering, or guaranteed fills. Raw outputs include queued ETH peaks/end, attempts/skips and heartbeat shortfall.

## Compared levers and interpretation

Keep 42 days as the existing default unless launch governance explicitly elects the separately tested 56-day drip **before** funding. A funded schedule freezes; changing Params afterward cannot accelerate or lengthen it. The slower drip reduces drawdown in these scenarios but does not establish better or assured week-2/3 payback. The tables compare both.

Ablations compare current-epoch first mining against next-epoch entry and the inflow regulator against the former payback/runway-only rule. Faster first rewards reduce waiting by up to six hours, which does not by itself fix aggregate cash payback. The seven-day policy preserves later reward inventory but still cannot manufacture ETH exits. A useful no-upfront-ETH route remains starter equipment → mining → token pack paid from pending credit (retained 10% token-pack discount plus 5% internal-credit bonus), with permissionless sponsored settlement/reveal. Existing tests cover both wallet payment and internal reward recycling. No additional token faucet or unbacked ETH promise is added.

Sniper cases are **separate** fresh-pool counterfactuals: a single 5 ETH buy at minute 1, 15 or 25. Taxes are exactly 4.5 / 2.5 / 1.25 ETH to the reward pool; no max-buy splitting. Immediate sale is shown with normal 2.25% fee and with the maximum 15% total sell fee to bracket progressive surcharge. No intervening traders or reward buys occur in this isolated round trip. Every case loses money in both brackets, but this is not proof a bot cannot profit if later buyers move price. The source preserves arbitrary buy volume and explicitly taxes timing.
