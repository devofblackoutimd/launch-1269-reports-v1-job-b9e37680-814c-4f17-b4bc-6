# v3.5 deployment and operations — no deployment authorized

This supersedes the changed portions of [v3.4's launch procedure](launch-v34.md). Existing deployed contracts and the existing token are untouched. No proxy, upgrade, migration or re-mint is introduced. There is no launch manifest in this submission.

## Parameters that must be supplied

Retain the accepted existing token (18 decimals), actual official PoolManager, actual initialization sender/launcher, immutable application factory, network fee recipient, tick spacing, swarm, Identity MD root/snapshot and reserved launch coordinates. These identities are constructor-bound exactly as in v3.4. Do not use rehearsal addresses. Retain 70% AMM / 10% swarm / 20% endowment, 42-day drip and the existing team address. The authenticated pool initialization controls all clocks. Verify real factory prediction, launcher support and predeployment reward allocation before a separately authorized launch. The historical guide's custody-free prediction and explicit funding fallback both remain applicable.

Pack profile is a deployment decision, not a fixed ETH price. `selectProfile(0)` selects Accessible (3/1.5/6 yield days, 3% hourly decay, 20 packs/hour); `1` selects Balanced (5/2.5/10, 2%, 20); `2` selects Scarce (7/3.5/14, 1%, 15). All retain the bounded Params sale step (default 1%). Selection can change until the first paid sale and then freezes. The swarm must select the requester-approved option; readiness is false before selection. The research recommends 1. Mean daily farm yield has a 3,000-token bootstrap floor; item pricing has a 0.000001-token/hash/epoch net-yield floor. Floors are economic assumptions to review before immutable deployment, not an ETH peg.

The constructor-only module registry reference is stored in storage to avoid a Solidity 0.8.26 immutable-patch assembly limit. It has no setter or upgrade/delegatecall path. Exact constructor bytecode and factory enrollment remain mandatory, so an alternate implementation cannot enroll. All modules take only `(registry)`; constructors make no external calls.

## Packaging: at most eight contracts per job

The modules are merged into fourteen roles, allowing two eight-contract application jobs:

| Application job | Ordered contracts |
|---|---|
| A (8) | Params, GameRegistry, RewardPool, FarmRewards, FarmCore, FarmItems, PackSale, StarterClaim |
| B (8) | FeeRouter, GridOperator, Season, FarmEngine, Economy, Enhancement, Civic, Billboards |

The custom V4Market hook and its flag-address factory belong to a separate infrastructure launch if they are not already supplied by the accepted token launch. That is the proposed third launch; do not exceed eight by adding infrastructure to A/B. Registry roles 0–13 are the module order above, excluding Params and GameRegistry. Registration remains permissionless, write-once, exact creation-code hash plus immutable factory prediction. `script/Deploy.s.sol` is a local rehearsal, not authority to broadcast. No name exceeds 32 characters and no library needs external linking.

Format, build, regenerate/check `src/lib/ModuleCode.sol`, then run `forge clean` and `forge build` and recheck pins before archiving bytecode or deriving launch addresses. A clean build prevents stale nested creation-code constants in cached prediction helpers. Register all fourteen roles before game operation. Bind exactly that registry's FeeRouter to the hook, fund the exact endowment and select the approved profile. Purchases open one hour after authenticated initialization only when all existing readiness checks pass. The ETH zap has the same gate and pause. Refresh `launch/V4Market.sol` using the provided flatten step; compile/deploy the archived canonical artifact, not separately compiled metadata.

## After launch

- Swarm: within launch +7 days call `StarterClaim.setFreeRoot(actualRoot)` once. Obtain the approved maximum-500 unique-wallet list from the requester, run `tools/freepack_merkle.py`, publish proofs and the root. Claims close at launch +30 days, independent of root publication time. Leaf is `keccak256(abi.encode(index,wallet))`, sorted-pair tree. The UI needs the generated `ui/allowlist.json` and actual registry address.
- Anyone/keeper: advance epochs; capture pack, starter, enhancement and season entropy using native chain block numbers; resolve/re-arm expired commitments. Re-arming preserves a paid attempt and does not refund, cancel or retry it. Keep the native-block keeper running with `--enhancement` set to the registered address. Enhancement cap changes require swarm announcement and permissionless activation, at least 48h notice and 30 days between raises; allowed sequence is 8→12→16.
- Anyone/keeper: `FeeRouter.sellBatchQuote()` returns the entire depth-limited eligible batch and minimum ETH. Execute exactly that amount with an equal or stricter minimum, at most once per five minutes. Depth cap is 0.1% of active concentrated liquidity's virtual token reserve, additionally capped at 0.1 ETH TWAP value. Thirty-minute average and 150 bps bound are enforced; failed fills revert queues and cadence. System sales pay the normal project/network fee without progressive sell surcharge. Already-capped heartbeat tokens stay in the pool; small favorable-execution ETH excess queues reward buys.
- Anyone/keeper: execute reward and season-pot buys using warmed 30-minute quotes, the 150 bps floor and shared five-minute buy cadence. Pot ETH is separately accounted. Batches are bounded by Params; the minimum is the smaller of 0.05 ETH and that queue. Execute a final smaller queue fully. The custom official V4Market supports system sells; the historical StandardAdapter is not an alternative v3.5 launch market.
- Players: locks cannot exit early. Repair/rooms/enhancement/cables/expansion spend wallet tokens or available credits; auto-repair uses credits only, skips unaffordable items and retries at later settlements. Farm actions recommit idle farms; idle epochs do not wear items. Sponsored settlement does not extend the owner's activity commitment.
- Swarm policy operator: supply seven completed daily wallet-funded token pack totals as `packSales7DaysTokens` to the dry-run `tools/regulator.py`; exclude pending-credit recycling. Its old ETH field is historical compatibility only. Pack profile target/decay are frozen after selection/first purchase, while Params DemandTarget still controls mining pace. No policy script signs or transmits transactions.
- Swarm: numeric Params changes remain within existing bounds and 10%/24h. `RepairHours` uses WAD hours (default `6 ether`, allowed 4–8); several old ETH quote keys remain ABI-compatible but no longer price v3.5 actions. The common enhancement/combo/set bonus ceiling defaults to +100% (bounded +80–100%). Locks remain additional redistribution weights, not emission creation.
- Keeper/players: settle a competition week before its seven-day grace closes; prepare once, capture entropy, finalize, then pull prizes. Best-farm rank requires 20 active epochs and spend capped at seven farm-yield days. Three distinct lottery winners use capped committed hash tickets. District rewards require paid spending in that week. Pot claims expire four weeks after the competition's settlement deadline; anyone rolls the residual into the current pot. Mining's separate 1% best-prize allocation is still credited at finalization. Full inventory reverts the entire item/pot claim and preserves entitlement.
- Anyone: settle billboard auctions after the display week begins and withdraw displaced bids. Bids during week W purchase display in W+1. Swarm may blank text, without redirecting bids. Text is 1–32 ASCII letters/digits/spaces/`!_-`, handle 1–15 ASCII alphanumerics/underscore; punctuation that permits links is rejected.

The 10% district-wallet cap is applied to each wallet's *credited influence* relative to uncapped district escrow, matching the prior district-scoring convention. Excess deposited principal remains fully refundable at maturity. A lone wallet receives 10% credited influence rather than making bootstrap mathematically impossible. The same convention applies to private war scoring. Locked principal counts toward grid stake until withdrawal, while its hash bonus expires at maturity.

Future native-block randomness remains sequencer-influenced, with no caller seed/cancellation and explicit asynchronous expiry handling. A production VRF migration would require a new immutable design and is outside this source-only change. Keepers, sell volume, liquidity and repair profitability are not guaranteed. Publish the real settings and operating responsibilities before release.
