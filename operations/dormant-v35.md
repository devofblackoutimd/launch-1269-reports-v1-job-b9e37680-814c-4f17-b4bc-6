# Restricted launch/operator notes: dormant modules

v3.7 authority update: every reference to the swarm below means SwarmDesk acting on a verified oracle answer. There is no signing wallet or direct operator command. The frozen weekly policy and age eligibility are in [launch-v37.md](../launch-v37.md); ordinary module gates remain as described here. Pack readiness uses the fixed Balanced profile, with no profile-selection transaction.

This file is an internal handoff, excluded from public site assets and public game documentation. Civic is registered at role 12 but all three optional modules begin disabled. No enable is executed by this assignment. Source and tests remain reviewable; hiding a UI does not hide on-chain bytecode.

Each module has a one-time swarm `announce(module,letter)` and permissionless `enable(module)` after 48 hours. IDs: 0 war, 1 collapse, 2 black market. An announced module cannot be re-announced and an enabled module cannot be disabled or upgraded. Announcements require nonempty bounded text. All token escrows use the existing token, exact balance checks, CEI and pull claims. There is no emergency owner withdrawal.

War starts only through the swarm, runs 48h, names two different districts and accepts contributions only from wallets in those districts. Credited chest influence per wallet is capped at 10% of that district's uncapped chest, with excess principal still part of settlement. The committed score combines credited chest divided by the snapshotted city-average daily rate (×10,000) and snapshotted active district hash. This explicit dimension conversion and hash snapshot prevent a delayed finalizer from choosing the outcome by changing later rates/hash. Ties select the first district. At finalization, winner wallets recover the entire winning chest plus half the losing chest pro rata to actual contributed tokens; the other half goes to RewardPool. If the selected winner has no contributors, all funds go to the pool. Claims are one per contributor; proportional integer dust remains escrowed, with no administrator withdrawal. Winner receives +10% output for seven days from finalization. A later war cannot start while the preceding one is unfinalized.

Grid collapse starts only through the swarm with a city-wide 24h goal at least one average-farm yield day. Contributions go directly to RewardPool and are not refundable. A met goal starts a one-day city-wide Green wave; a missed goal schedules exactly one city-wide outage epoch. Finalization is permissionless after the deadline. The outage still honors item off-grid mechanics, and rewards stay within the reserved emission envelope. Epoch snapshots consume the forced event; no per-farm keeper loop is introduced.

Black market: swarm lists one lot per seven-day week, supply 1–3, kind 0–11, rarity 1–3 (Rare/Epic/Legendary). Current-week purchases only; the same launch clock, funded-endowment, binding and explicit-profile readiness checks as packs apply. Price descends linearly from 10 to 2 city-average yield days over the week and follows the last settled rate with the same floor. All proceeds go to RewardPool. A paid purchase counts toward first-paid provenance unlock. Inventory failures, insufficient allowance, wrong week, exhausted supply and active purchase pauses revert atomically. Normal PackSale pause (at most 72h, auto-expiry and cooldown) covers these buys. It does not pause war funding/claims, collapse funding or mining.

The public seasonal district promotion is also administered through Civic: at most once per season, one district, 0–15% output, seven-day duration. It is snapshotted by GridOperator and combined with any war boost; the maximum combined district boost is 25%. Rewards reserve a 150% gross-output envelope to cover that and the 15% maximum Green output. The boost redistributes within reserved mining emission; no tokens are minted.

War/collapse/market activation, goals, lot choice and promotion choice are bounded oracle fields in v3.7. The immutable weekly question defines the initial proposal policy; no human operator can override it. Independent tests cover disabled calls, premature activation, repeated activation, contribution custody, immutable score snapshots, payout conservation, city result consumption, auction price/supply/expiry and pause behavior.
